## 🧩 Business Problem

> 🏪 **Retail Company Issue:**  
> The business has a centralized data warehouse but lacks unified reporting, deep customer profiling, and product performance tracking. 

You built a scalable **Analytics System** to solve:

- No customer or product performance tracking  
- Lack of segmentation and behavioral insights
- No advanced reporting or KPI tracking  

The result is a **clean, actionable suite of analytical reports and business insights**.

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project demonstrates how to query and analyze an enterprise data environment using SQL.  
It includes:

### ✅ 1. EDA + Advanced SQL Data Analysis
Using advanced SQL analytics techniques:
- Ranking  
- Segmentation  
- Cumulative metrics  
- Change-over-time analysis  
- Performance metrics  
- Customer & Product reports  
- KPI calculations  
- Exploratory Data Analysis insights  

### ✅ 2. Advanced Reporting
SQL-based reports extracting business value:
- **Customer Analytics Report**
- **Product Performance Report**

---

## 🛠️ Key Features

* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Comprehensive dimension, metadata, and measure exploration.
* **Advanced SQL Analytics:** Implementation of ranking, segmentation frameworks, time-series, and cumulative performance trends.
* **Business Metric Tracking:** KPI calculations including Recency, Average Order Value (AOV), and Average Monthly Spend.

---

## 📊 Reports Generated

### 📘 Customer Analytics Report
* **Segmentation:** VIP, Regular, and New customers
* **Engagement Metrics:** Recency, Lifespan, Avg Order Value (AOV), and Avg Monthly Spend
* **Sales Aggregations:** Total orders, total products, quantity purchased, and total sales

### 📙 Product Performance Report
* **Segmentation:** High Performer, Mid-Range, and Low Performer product lines
* **Engagement Metrics:** Recency, Lifespan, and Avg Selling Price
* **Sales Aggregations:** Unique customer count and Monthly revenue
